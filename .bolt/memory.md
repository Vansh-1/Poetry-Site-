# Project memory

## Product intent & audience

- **The Poetry Room** — a poetry website/platform built with Vite + React.
- Tagline: "A Home for Every Feeling" — positions the site as an inclusive emotional space for poetic expression.
- Audience likely values introspection, literary aesthetics, and emotional resonance.

## Brand personality & voice

- Warm, tender, and literary.
- Emphasizes emotional inclusivity ("every feeling").
- Avoids clinical or overly technical language in user-facing copy.

## Visual design & UX direction

- Favicon: golden feather resting on an open book — literary, delicate motif.
- Implied palette: warm golds and soft tones; book/paper textures likely welcome.
- Aesthetic leans minimalist-poetic rather than dense or corporate.
- Motifs to prefer: feathers, open books, pages, soft ink-like accents.

## Decisions, constraints & rationale

- Site title set to `The Poetry Room — A Home for Every Feeling` in `index.html`.
- Favicon implemented as a feather-on-book icon (SVG-based, golden).
- Stack: Vite + React (entry point and `index.html` in standard Vite layout).
- Rationale: the favicon visually reinforces the poetry/literary identity and the emotional-home concept.

## Open questions & assumptions

- Assumption: site is single-purpose poetry experience; no multi-author or CMS features confirmed yet.
- Unresolved: full color palette, typography choices, layout structure, and content sections not yet specified.
- Unresolved: whether the site will host user-submitted poems or be curated/static.
